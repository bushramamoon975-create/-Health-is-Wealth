A refreshing traditional Pakistani drink!

🌸 How to Make Lassi

Milk and yogurt can be combined to make a delicious homemade lassi.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • Some cold water
  • Optional: a little sugar

Preparation

Add yogurt and milk to a blender. Add a little cold water and blend until smooth and creamy.

Health Benefits

Yogurt and milk provide calcium and protein. Calcium supports healthy bones and teeth.

Healthy Tip 💚

Enjoy homemade lassi as part of a balanced diet. Use less added sugar for an everyday drink.

🌸 Natural Skin Care Tips

🌹 Rose Face Mask & Popular Skin Care Tips

Click the button below to open rose face mask recipes and popular everyday skin care tips.

1. Quick Rose & Honey Glow Mask

Ingredients: 1 tbsp pure rosewater (or 1 tsp rose petal powder) + 1 tsp raw honey.
How to use: Mix and apply for 10–15 minutes, then rinse with lukewarm water for soft, hydrated skin.

2. Classic Rose & Yogurt Glow Mask

Ingredients: 2 tbsp crushed rose petals (or 1 tbsp rose powder), 1 tbsp plain yogurt, 1 tsp raw honey.
How to use: Apply an even layer to clean skin for 15 minutes, then rinse gently with lukewarm water.

3. Rosewater, Sandalwood & Besan Pack

Ingredients: 1 tbsp besan (gram flour), ½ tbsp sandalwood powder, a pinch of wild turmeric, 1–2 tbsp pure rosewater.
How to use: Leave on for 10–12 minutes until semi-dry, then rinse off with cool water.

4. Instant Rose & Aloe Hydrating Gel

Ingredients: 1 tbsp pure aloe vera gel, 1 tbsp rosewater, ½ tsp vegetable glycerin (optional).
How to use: Apply a thick layer for 15–20 minutes, then rinse with cool water before moisturizing.

⭐ Popular Everyday Tips People Love

  • Chilled Rosewater Ice Cubes: Freeze pure rosewater in an ice tray and gently rub a cube wrapped in a soft cloth over tired or warm skin for instant freshness.
  • Simple 3-Step Night Routine: Wash your face gently, apply a light moisturizer, and use lip balm before bed instead of complicated steps.
  • Water & Sunscreen Daily: Drink enough water throughout the day and wear sunscreen outdoors to support natural radiance.
💡 Important Reminder

Always do a quick patch test on your jawline or inner wrist 24 hours before applying any homemade mask to your full face.

🥛 Milk Malai for Skin Moisture

Yes, milk malai (fresh cream) helps moisturize the skin because it contains natural fats and soothing dairy compounds that leave dry or rough skin feeling softer and temporarily more hydrated.

How to Use Milk Malai

  • How much: Take a small amount of fresh malai (about ½ to 1 teaspoon).
  • How to apply: Smooth it onto clean skin, leave it on for 10–15 minutes, and rinse off gently with lukewarm or cool water.
  • Benefit: Helps soften dry patches and add a smooth feel.

A Few Things to Keep in Mind

  • Oily or acne-prone skin: Heavy cream can clog pores or trigger breakouts on skin that is already oily or prone to acne. If your skin leans oily, skip heavy layers or try a lighter option like rosewater, plain yogurt, or a non-comedogenic moisturizer.
  • Use it fresh: Always use fresh malai rather than old or sour dairy, which can irritate the skin.
  • Patch test first: Test a small dab along your jawline or inner wrist before applying it all over your face to make sure it doesn't cause irritation or bumps.

🌿 Benefits of Fenugreek Leaves

Fresh Fenugreek Leaves and Their Benefits

Fenugreek leaves, also known as Methi, are nutritious green leaves with many health benefits.

🌱 Health Benefits of Methi

1. Improves Digestion

Fenugreek leaves contain dietary fiber that supports healthy digestion.

2. Supports Blood Sugar

The fiber in fenugreek may help slow down sugar absorption.

3. Supports Heart Health

Fenugreek leaves provide fiber that contributes to a heart-healthy diet.

4. Supports Strong Bones

They contain vitamin K, which helps maintain healthy bones.

5. Provides Iron

They contain iron, which helps the body make red blood cells.

6. Provides Antioxidants

They contain antioxidants that help protect the body's cells.

🍲 How to Eat Fenugreek Leaves

  • 🥘 Potato Methi Curry
  • 🫓 Methi Paratha
  • 🥣 Methi Dal
  • 🥗 Healthy Vegetable Meals
⚠️ Important Note

Enjoy fenugreek leaves as part of a balanced diet. If you take diabetes medication, ask a healthcare professional before using concentrated fenugreek supplements.

👁️ Eye Care Tips

Simple Ways to Care for Your Eyes While Using Mobile & Laptop Screens.

💚 Protect Your Eyes from Screens

Mobile phones and laptops are part of our daily lives. Taking regular breaks and following healthy screen habits can help reduce eye strain.

🌿 1. Follow the 20-20-20 Rule

Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds.

💧 2. Blink Your Eyes

Remember to blink regularly while using screens. This helps keep your eyes comfortable.

💡 3. Adjust Screen Brightness

Keep your screen brightness comfortable. Avoid using very bright screens in dark rooms.

📱 4. Keep a Comfortable Distance

Keep your mobile and laptop at a comfortable viewing distance. Avoid holding your phone too close to your face.

🌞 5. Take Regular Breaks

Take short breaks during long screen sessions. Stand up, move around and give your eyes a rest.

💻 6. Adjust Text Size

Increase text size if you need to squint or lean forward to read your screen.

😴 7. Get Enough Sleep

Maintain a regular sleep routine to support your overall eye health and wellbeing.

💚 Healthy Eye Care Reminder

Give your eyes regular breaks, blink often and keep your screen at a comfortable distance.

⚠️ Important

If you experience persistent eye pain, blurred vision or frequent headaches, consult an eye care professional.
